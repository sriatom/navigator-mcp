Arista EVPN Fabric

Two Arista EVPN/VXLAN fabrics (PD and QA) joined through a WAN border fabric that acts as the EVPN domain gateway, with a firewall in front of each fabric and an ISP behind the border. 22 cEOS nodes, for health checks, path questions and QA-only change tests.

Routing policy

This decides which traffic crosses a firewall. Each fabric gets its default route only through its own firewall, but every specific route the WAN exports (RT 10301:64999) is imported straight into PCS and QACS. So traffic leaving a fabric skips its firewall when the destination is known, and traffic entering a fabric always goes through that fabric's firewall.

Quirks in the lab (good test material)

  • QAFW1 Ethernet1/2 descriptions say WANFAB1A/1B:Eth8; they are cabled to QASVCL1A/1B Eth7.
  • PDSPINE2 Eth5 says SPINE2-LEAF3A and its BGP neighbor says PDLEAF3A; it is PDSVCL1A. QASPINE2 has the same with QALEAF3A.
  • On WANFAB1A/1B, the BGP descriptions for 10.25.10.32 and 10.25.10.132 say …1A_OL; they are the 1B service leafs.
  • WANFAB Eth9 says SERVICE1:Eth1 (it is SERVICE3); SERVICE3 Eth2 says PDLEAF2B:eth5 (it is WANFAB1B Eth9).
  • PDLEAF2A/2B Eth5 and Po21 name SERVICE1 / CSR1; the node there is SERVICE2.
  • The image tag is ceos:4336m, but the nodes report EOS 4.35.2F.
